LittleEasy is a new learning app for younger South African learners, from Grade R through Grade 9. It comes from EASIFIEDCORP (Pty) Ltd, the company behind Easified, a free CAPS study app for Grade 10 to 12.

It is being built so learning can feel friendly and clear, in a way that suits children, parents and teachers. More news will follow here as LittleEasy takes shape.
